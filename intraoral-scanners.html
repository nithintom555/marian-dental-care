About Intraoral Scanners

An intraoral scanner uses a small handheld device to capture digital images of the teeth and surrounding tissues. These images can help create digital models for selected restorations, aligners and other treatment planning. The dentist can explain whether a digital scan is suitable for your needs.

Availability and suitability of any procedure or technology should be confirmed directly with Marian Dental Care.
